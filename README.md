# Mayur..This Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) report concerns a 56-year-old male patient weighing 50 kg (initials SM) who developed skin erosions and edema on August 1, 2026, after taking 3 to 6 medications following a head injury accident. The primary suspected drug is oral Phenytoin 100 mg, administered three times daily starting on August 1, 2026, which was discontinued on August 2, 2026, upon appearance of the reaction. Following the prompt discontinuation of Phenytoin, hospitalization, and the provision of supportive care, the adverse reaction successfully subsided.
